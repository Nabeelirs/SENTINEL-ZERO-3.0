# Judge demonstration — 3 to 5 minutes

This is a live technical walkthrough. It does not replace a separately required
short submission video/deck or completion of the NASA submission form.

## 0:00–0:35 — the problem and real observation

“A satellite difference is the start of an investigation. SENTINEL ZERO connects
what the radar measured with the evidence needed to inspect and trust a report.”

Open the Earth Atlas. Identify the exploratory Sri Lankan window, October 6 and
18, actual NASA–ISRO NISAR PROVISIONAL observations. Explain that this is one
processed study, not a worldwide processed map or confirmed environmental event.

## 0:35–1:15 — compare and inspect

Switch Before → After → Change → Compare. Move the aligned divider; both source
dates use the same scale. Purple/teal indicate lower/greater backscatter power,
not flood classes. Display limits saturate colours only, not saved values.

Start Investigation and choose the largest absolute-change cell. Native row 157,
column 309 returns −26.151365 dB at E387095/N766775 m. Mention exact eligibility
and unresolved physical cause; descriptive magnitude thresholds are exploratory.

## 1:15–2:35 — challenge and trace evidence

Open Challenge Evidence. Show the separate local-integrity/scientific-quality/
physical-cause statuses. Create an isolated statistics copy, modify it, and verify
MISMATCH. “This is our disposable copy; NASA sources remain unchanged.”

Open Evidence Twin. Select SZ-006 statistics. Show the actual downstream analysis
manifest and Evidence Passport requiring revalidation. The graph traces evidence
dependencies, not environmental consequences or real-world damage estimates.

## 2:35–3:30 — reconstruct and carry evidence forward

Open Restore & Report. Restore bytes, then Rebuild statistics from verified
inputs. Distinguish copying baseline bytes from scientific recomputation. Show
MATCH/numerical readback without claiming uncertainty is removed. Download the
Evidence Passport; its maps use protected measurements and its trust state
reflects the current demonstration. Technical JSON remains optional.

## 3:30–4:30 — global reach and honest limits

Open Global Explorer, choose a researched study window or bounded coordinates
and dates, then explicitly search NASA CMR. Show actual returned granules/footprints
and candidate compatibility checks. No records is an honest result, not a reason
to substitute Sri Lankan imagery. Catalog discovery is not completed global
processing. If offline, explain the network requirement and retain the executable
pilot instead of presenting cached results as a fresh search.

Close with the innovation: “Inspect the measurement. Trace its dependencies.
Challenge the evidence. Protect the decision.” Rainfall context, additional
regions and stronger external assurance retain their documented missing evidence.
