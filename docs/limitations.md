# Limitations

- **PROVISIONAL** observations: joint-mode 40 MHz banding, RFI and other artifacts
  may remain. Finite-pixel counts do not settle quality.
- Provider QA/ISO and original HDF5 attributes are incompletely inspected.
- Original-to-subset numerical fidelity and empirical physical co-registration
  are unverified. Matching declared grids establish a narrower result.
- The focusing mask is not universal quality assurance; looks are not a complete
  uncertainty model. No invented confidence percentage or accuracy score.
- The exploratory window is not an authoritative full Kelani Basin boundary.
- Radar response changes do not establish flood, damage, land-cover change or
  cause. Projected cell footprint is not flood extent or actual surface area.
- Worldwide catalog discovery is operational; worldwide validated processing is not.
- Original usable rainfall observations are unavailable. Missing data do not mean
  no rainfall. Uploaded context remains owner-declared, not provider-authenticated.
- Local hashes establish recorded consistency; a demo mismatch is not a NASA attack.
- Public HTTPS, Linux installation, concurrency and host capacity require their
  own acceptance. Local rendering does not establish public availability.
- Mobile scrolls vertically; the native square is never stretched. No pinch
  gesture or comprehensive assistive-technology certification is claimed.
- Licensing, contributor rights and third-party/data/image reuse must be cleared.
  Software completion does not submit to NASA or guarantee a competition result.
